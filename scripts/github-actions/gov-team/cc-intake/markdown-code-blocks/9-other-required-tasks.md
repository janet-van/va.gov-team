## Other required tasks

Depending on your project, your team may need to reach out to these other teams for support.

- **Content and information architecture:** The Content and IA team will contact you about how they'll support your team's product content. You'll also need to work with the Content and IA team if your initiative requires any new or updated content in the Drupal CMS. If you have questions, reach out to them in their channel, [#content-ia-centralized-team](https://dsva.slack.com/archives/C01K37HRUAH).
- **Research:** If you're doing research with Veterans, a [Research Review](https://depo-platform-documentation.scrollhelp.site/collaboration-cycle/research-review) is required.
- **Analytics:** If making any visible changes on VA.gov, a [Google Analytics Request](https://depo-platform-documentation.scrollhelp.site/collaboration-cycle/analytics-request) is required.
- **Call Center Product Guides:** If you need to create or update your Product Guide for Veteran Call Centers, a [Contact Center Review](https://depo-platform-documentation.scrollhelp.site/collaboration-cycle/contact-center-review) is required.
