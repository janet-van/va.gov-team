## Product context

- The **21-0538** (Mandatory Verification of Dependents) is a paper form that is used to verify the dependents on a Veteran's disability benefit only. Veterans are required to submit this paper form every 8 years. This team was tasked with turning the form into a lightweight online tool. Preliminary research on a lightweight prototype was completed in Feb/Mar 2025\. The MVP prototype only captures a "no changes to my dependents" answer.  
  - [About VA Form 21-0538 (Mandatory Verification of Dependents)](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-21-0538/)  
  - \~114,000 paper forms submitted per year, as of 2025  
  - Current pdf version on VA.gov: JAN 2024

### Product Goal

Make the online dependent experience as easy to complete and cohesive as possible, while ensuring that Veterans verify dependent information on a regular basis and that submissions are reliable and processed as quickly as possible.

- The long term goal is that the VA can eventually get rid of this process altogether (as has been done for the Veterans Pension program) because Veterans have other more reliable and less confusing means to keep their dependents updated.  
- Until then, our goal is to allow Veterans to come to the dependent management area of VA.gov and verify their dependents (21-0538). Many of the dependent forms ask for the same information, so there is a lot of opportunity to leverage a single form experience to prefill information and reduce the burden on the Veteran to manage their dependents.  
- We decided to just release the “no changes” version of the 0538 as MVP. If a user has changes to make, they have to use the 686/674, and then later return to the 0538 to submit ‘no changes’.This is because the paper 0538 as designed does not allow users to make ALL changes they might need to make (they can’t report a change in student status, remove a parent due to death, or add dependents). Our thinking was that users could think the 0538 displayed all possible options and thus not take an action they might actually need to take on a dependent, resulting in under or over-payments.   
- We think that integrating the 0538 with the existing 686 would lead to: easier navigation for users, less confusion about the process, and more complete and comprehensive verification of dependents. This proposal is captured in this [mural](https://app.mural.co/t/departmentofveteransaffairs9999/m/departmentofveteransaffairs9999/1745620624724/70ee36c200a3b66b426c33aa189dcdcc5a4a0192?wid=0-1763522503702).
