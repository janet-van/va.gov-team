### **Summary of Overall Feedback**

The majority of Veterans rated the VA Health & Benefits mobile app highly across feedback mechanisms and in App and Play store reviews described it positively as being easy to use and convenient.

Negative feedback is in the minority with users continuing to report struggles largely with sign-in and some issues with app reliability. In contrast to January, reviewers generally praised the navigation and there were few negative comments about finding specific features. Suggestions for improvement echo previous months' in desiring more information in appointments and an easier and more efficient way to renew prescriptions. New this month are users wanting the ability to order medical supplies.

## **Common Themes for Issues**

### **1\. Login and basic reliability issues prevent users from using the app (\~21 reviews)**

Users continue to experience difficulty getting into the app with issues such as looping back to login, failed identity verification, requiring multi-factor authentication even after successful previous logins. General failures to open the app or have it functioning smoothly adds to user frustration and prevents them from using the app at all.

**Representative responses:**

* "can't sign on for over a month tried everything nothing works. very poor support."  
* "since the last update I've been unable to log in it puts in a loop where it sends me to the third party id verification and then tries to log me in then send some back to the third party verification."  
* “why have the biometric sign in option if I still have to sign in the long way every time?”  
* “The app isn't working today\!\!\! Ugh.”


**Pain points:** 

* As a Veteran, I want to login quickly and reliably to the mobile app, but I am blocked by login and authentication issues.  
* As a Veteran, I want the app to work reliably after updates, but I am blocked by the app no longer working properly after updates, including crashes and extended loading times.

**Recommendation:** Investigate and stabilize the login flow and post-update stability through a combination of 1\. user research to drill down into what users are experiencing, 2\. technical investigation to identify when technical issues vs UX or user error issues are presenting, 3\. improve error handling and loading states

### **2\. Users expect additional functionality within the app to enable them to easily complete essential tasks (\~10 reviews)**

Users want to be able to fully manage essential benefits within the app, not just pieces of the journey, including:

* Making/rescheduling appointments    
* Ordering medical supplies in addition to prescriptions  
* Deleting messages no longer needed and more customizable content management like hiding providers no longer used  
* Sending renewal requests to providers when requesting refills for expired prescriptions 

**Representative responses:**

* "There is a trash folder but apparently no way to move old messages into the trash. I have messages from years ago that I can't figure out how to get out of the way so I can only look at the new important ones\!\!"  
* "VA you did it with this app\! The only thing I can see you can do to this app is allow us to schedule appointments. I think it would be to difficult to do that….. Just a thought\! Great job VA on this one\!"  
* "There needs to be a way to refill prescriptions easier than having to write messages if they need to be renewed. We should be able to request refills and get them automatically refilled or requested to be renewed by the doctor."

**Pain points:**

* As a Veteran who uses secure messages, I want to delete old secure messages so I can focus on new and relevant messages, but I am blocked by the app not providing me the ability to delete.  
* **UPDATED:** As a Veteran who has medical appointments, I want to manage all appointment based tasks directly in the app, but am blocked by my inability to book, cancel, **and reschedule** appointments in the app.

Recommendation: Provide users with expanded capabilities to better meet their needs in completing the full scope of tasks such as appointment making and deleting secure messages.

### **3\. Missing expected information and artifacts cause frustration and confusion (\~10 reviews)**

Veterans expect and want:

* Additional details about appointments including purpose of visit and provider name  
* Up-to-date and reliably visible letters and documents, prescriptions information

**Representative responses:**

* "Why do you list only the day, time, and building for all appointments in the app, phone and emails? Why can you not include what the appointment is for and who it is with?"  
* "I can’t see any of my prescriptions in the app. can't see what scrip are being refilled or what may need refilled. always sez no prescriptions found\! these are as important as appointments. plus appointments information is never complete.  
* “This app is fairly easy to use. But sometimes looking at your letters, they won’t come up when you click to do that.”

**Pain points:**

* As a Veteran with medical appointments, I want to have detailed information about my appointments so I can adequately prepare for them, but I am blocked by not having enough appointment details such as provider name or the issue I am scheduled to be seen for.  
* As a Veteran, I want to access my VA letters and documents (claims decision letters, payment history, disability letters) in the app, but I am blocked by letters being missing, payment history only loading for certain years, and incorrect dates on printed letters.  
* **NEW**: As a Veteran, I want to reliably see my prescription information so I can manage my medications, but I am blocked by prescription information not displaying reliably. 

**Recommendation:** Provide additional key information for appointments such as purpose of visit and provider to better support Veterans' health management. Investigate why not all features’ information is up-to-date and displaying for users and ensure information provided for each feature is correct and displaying correctly.

### **Navigation**

**Average rating:** 4.14 | **\# of ratings:** 114

Most Veterans found navigation intuitive, with a minority finding it difficult and time consuming. Two users commented on sign out with one wanting to be able to do so more easily and another suggesting it be available on the homepage.  

**Representative responses:**

* "I love how crisp this app looks. It's streamlined and really easy to get where you need to go."  
* "Too difficult and time consuming to follow many unnecessary menus.”  
* “In order to log out, currently the process is as such: Hit 'Profile', Hit 'Settings', Hit 'Log Out'. Is there an alternate and easier sequence for us to take to log out?”

### **Login**

**Average rating:** 2.5 | **\# of ratings:** 26

Login continues to be the most problematic area. Many Veterans who commented about login had issues with authentication or being repeatedly looped back to the login screen.

**Representative responses:**

* "for some reason the app gets stuck in a verification at sign in. I used both [Login.gov](http://Login.gov) and ID to verify and it dumps me out back at the sign in page. this is even after app update."  
* "initially this app worked great and was real convenient to check appointments and refill medications. Now you can't even sign in and if you do get lucky it will take numerous attempts. What once was a great app is no longer worth the time."

This month saw a dip in prescriptions rating, mostly due to a minority of users who gave ratings of 1-2 due to struggles with refills and renewals or being able to view their refills at all in the app. The majority of reviewers were enthusiastic about the feature and found it easy to use. 

**Representative responses:**

* “When requesting med refills or renews you must first go to the scrip page and write down the med info and then go to the messages section to then enter the data.”  
* “Makes keeping up with your prescriptions so easy”

### **Appointments** 

**Average rating:** 3.75 | **\# of ratings:** 8

There were a [record number of appointment detail views](https://github.com/department-of-veterans-affairs/va.gov-team/tree/master/products/va-mobile-app/analytics#mobile-app-health-data-trends) in app this month compared to previous months \- a jump to 12,867,747 views from 8,584,358 views in January. Reviewers liked how easy it is to keep track of appointments, but some repeated previous feedback on wanting more details on appointment purpose and the ability to reschedule appointments. 

**Representative responses:**

* “Feature Request: please enable users to Reschedule their appointments in the app. Issue: if a user cannot make an appointment, they need to reschedule it. However, the app only allows users to cancel the appointment. When an appointment is cancelled, there is no follow up from the VA, the user has to spend an hour on the phone trying to reach the right person to schedule the appointment again, and sometimes requires VA staff or do more paperwork by ordering another consult (for a specialty clinic).”  
* “I love the app and would give it 5 stars, but, it’s mission one feature:It needs to show you what the appointment is for. When you have a lot of appointments stacked, it makes it very hard to keep track of what the appointment is for which makes it difficult to prep.If this feature was added, this would be 6/5 stars.”  
* “Easy to use and keeps all my appointments to integrate with my Google calendar.”

## **Feature Enhancements**

**Feature requests across February 2026 included:**

* Additional information in appointments including purpose of visit and provider name to better allow Veterans to prepare for and manage appointments  
* Ability to reschedule appointments  
* Easier workflow to request prescription renewals  
* Ability to order medical supplies such as hearing aids  
* Sign out should be easier to find such as by moving it to the homepage

**Representative responses:**

* “NOT ENOUGH INFORMATION\! Calling Whole Health and Scheduling did not help. Why do you list only the day, time, and building for all appointments in the app, phone and emails? Why can you not include what the appointment is for and who it is with?”  
* “Feature Request: please enable users to Reschedule their appointments in the app.”  
* “When you need to get your prescriptions and there are zero refills there is no way to submit a refill without calling it in to pharmacy. Why can’t you have the app send a note to doctor when you hit button to submit refill, instead of making us call and listen to a 5 minute recorded message before we can submit our refill ‘for doctors approval’.”  
* “Why can’t you refill hearing aid and CPAP supplies through the app\!?”  
* “Sign out should be in the Home page”
