## Background 🏗️
<details><summary>Medical Calculators was released nationally in June 2024. This is a periodic check-in to engage with current users via an open ended format to help identify pain points and opportunities to consider for the continued improvement of the product and learn how we can increase usage.. </summary>

	 
</details>

## Research Goals 🥅	

### Goals

`1.` Identify top calculators clinicians are using and what is working well.

`2.` Understand barriers to use or friction points.

`3.` Identify new calculators that would support clinical workflows.  
  
### Outcome


**This research will help us learn what pain points clinicians are experiencing when using Medical Calculators and what improvements would best support their workflow and clinical decision making.** 

---

### Research questions

**`1.`What are the top barriers or points of friction that impact the clinician's experience using CDS Medical Calculators?** 

**`2.`What improvements or new calculators would best support clinician's workflows?** 

**`3.`What are clinicians using today in CDS Medical Calculators and what is working well?**


### Hypotheses

---
**`1.`Clinicians only use a handful of calculators and we already have developed the ones that are most needed (besides PREVENT).**
       
**`2.`Clinicians find the workflow to get to Medical Calcualtors has too many clicks.**
      
**`3.`Many clinicians are still not aware of the CDS Console.**


---
  
## Methodology  🛠️


</details>

**Interview Sessions**

`1.`Participants will be asked some interview questions about their role at the VA and their current or past use of the CDS Medical Calculator application. 

`2.`Clinicians will be asked questions from the conversation guide.

`3.`Clinicians will share their screen and production environment as needed to identify issues or pain point as well as point out features. Since it will be production (real patient data) we will not record these sessions.

**Async via Email**
`1.`Participants can choose to respond via email to provided questions if they prefer and do not have time for a 1:1 remote session.

### Outreach Email Templates
**General template, adjustments made for individual emails based on context or prior connection with participant. The outreach email must contain the line about voluntary participation and anonymity**

Hello (Name),
My name is Rosemary Rogers, and I am part of the Medical Calculators team. You previously reached out about [insert topic here] via our support form which is how we have your contact information.

I’m reaching out today because we are looking for ways to improve the Medical Calculators experience and would love to hear from you! Your participation is voluntary and any information you share will be anonymized when we document our learnings.

**There are two ways to participate:**
1.	We can schedule a 1:1 session for 30 or 60 minutes to discuss your current experience.
a.	If you choose option 1 – reply to this email with some days and times that typically work well for you over the next few weeks and skip the questions in the next section since we’ll cover them in our chat.
2.	You can participate via email by responding to the questions in the next section.
a.	If you choose option 2 – reply to this email and scroll down to place your answers in line with the questions in a different color.


**Questions for email participation**
1.	What is your usage of Medical Calculators?
a.	Explorer: I’m aware of Medical Calculators, and I’ve poked around. I have not used it in my clinical decision making.
b.	Intermediate: I have used Medical Calculators off and on for clinical decision making, but it is not part of my regular workflow.
c.	Advanced: I’ve fully integrated Medical Calculators into my regular workflows when providing patient care.
2.	What is your biggest barrier or pain point with using CDS Medical Calculators? Share as much detail as possible.
3.	Based on your experience so far with the CDS Medical Calculator application, what would you want us to prioritize going forward? Rank the following and add your own idea if you have something else in mind.
a.	Add more calculators (if so, which ones?). 
b.	Add more clinical guidance with recommended actions based on calculator results? (Which calculators would you prioritize adding guidance to?)
c.	Enhance the application’s ability to recommend the right calculator at the right time.
d.	Provide more guidance within the application for users on what specific functionality exists.
4.	We have several calculators that do not get used frequently (such as NIH Stroke Scale, Well's PE, TIMI STEMI, TIMI NSTEMI). Why do you think this is?
5.	How did you learn about CDS and the Medical Calculator application?
6.	How familiar do you think your colleagues are with the CDS Console and the Medical Calculator application? In your opinion, what would help increase awareness?


We really appreciate your insight and engagement. It will help us continue to improve the tools to work best for clinicians! Please don’t hesitate to reach out with any questions.

Cheers,
Rosemary

### Notetaker and Silent Obeserver Guide
- Be a silent observer. If you have a question for the participant, ask the facilitator via a Slack DM and they'll accommodate it if appropriate.
- If you can notetake, please do! It helps researchers a lot to see what you captured. There’s a note taking template in this Mural with screenshots. If you find it easier to write your notes or use a word doc, that's fine too! If you take running notes, please be sure to make a clear reference to the screen in question or the scenario being mentioned.
- Write what you hear and see as much as possible. For some it can be easier to write in first person, as it is said, so that you don't need to paraphrase anything. Try to capture what they said without adding your own interpretation of what they meant. E.g. "I'm not sure about this checkbox" does not equal "Doesn't understand checkbox."
- Share your notes back with the facilitator via Slack if taken outside of the mural. Do not include ANY PII in this chat.
- For design testing, try to write down things that aren't said—for instance, when someone misunderstood some copy or clicked the wrong element. It helps us figure out what needs to be revisited.
- Share a few raw impressions with the facilitator via Slack or in the notetaking document after the interview is done, if possible. It helps with synthesis!

**Protecting PII**
- It's important to protect the privacy of our VHA staff participants AND any Veterans. 
- Do not include the participants name or other identifiers -- such as a very specific title that can be easy to identify them with in your notes. Instead, use a general role description, such as "primary care provider" and "also works in informatics"
- In the event a participant needs to share their screen or a tool with an actual patient (since test patients don't always work to show how the tool functions), stop the recording before they share. DO NOT RECORD THE PARTICIPANT'S SCREEN IF THEY ARE SHOWING A REAL PATIENT.
