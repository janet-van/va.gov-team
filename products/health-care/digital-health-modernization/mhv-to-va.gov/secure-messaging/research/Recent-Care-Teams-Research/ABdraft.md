# Conversation Guide: Recent Care Teams Research

---

## Pain points this guide is designed to investigate

| # | Pain Point | Description | Where addressed |
|---|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Flow complexity** | Extra steps and multi-page interactions feel "over-engineered" and slow message composition | Warm-up Q5; Task 1 (interstitial, step count wrap-up); Task 2 post-probes; Task 3 post-probes; Comparative Q1–3 |
| 2 | **Unmet "recent care team" needs** | ~25–30% of users immediately click "Select a different care team," indicating the initial options often don't match expectations | Warm-up Q4; Task 1 (recent care teams page, "team not listed" scenario); Task 2 (recent teams observation); Task 3 (recent teams observation); Comparative Q4–6 |
| 3 | **Provider confusion** | Users find it difficult to locate previously messaged providers among care systems and care teams | Warm-up Q3; Task 1 (select a care team page, contact list); Task 2 (filter & combo box); Task 3 (care system → care team); Comparative Q7–9 |

## Warm-Up – 5 minutes

Before we look at anything on screen, I'd like to learn a little about how you communicate with your VA health care providers.

1. Can you tell me a bit about how you currently communicate with your VA care teams? Do you use secure messaging, phone, in-person visits, or something else?

2. About how often do you send secure messages? Is it usually to the same team, or do you message different teams?

3. How many different VA care teams or providers do you communicate with? Can you describe them briefly?
   - *Probe:* Are they at the same VA facility or different ones? Different specialties?
   - *Probe:* If someone asked you to explain the difference between a "care system" and a "care team," how would you describe it?

4. When you need to start a new message, how do you decide which care team to send it to? How do you find them?
   - *Probe:* Are the providers you message most often easy to find, or do you have to search for them?
   - *Probe:* Have you ever started a message and the team you wanted to reach wasn't in the list you first saw? What did you do?

Great, thanks for that context. Now let's take a look at the real thing.

## Task 2: Start a new message (scenario-driven)

> **Moderator note:** Use the scenario below to give the Veteran a realistic reason to compose a message. This tests the full current flow naturally — interstitial, recent care teams, care team selection, categories, and compose — through the Veteran's actual behavior rather than page-by-page Q&A.

**Scenario:** Imagine you recently had some lab work done and you got your results back, but one of the numbers looks a little off to you. You want to send a message to your doctor's team to ask what it means and whether you need to do anything. Go ahead and show me how you would do that.

*[Let them lead. Do not tell them where to click. Probe at each page as they naturally arrive there.]*

As you go through this, please think aloud, tell me what you're looking at, what you're thinking, and what you expect to happen.

---

### Interstitial page

> **Pain point 1 (flow complexity):** Does this page feel like a useful step or a barrier to getting to the message?

*[When they land on the interstitial, pause them briefly:]*

- What do you notice first on this screen?
- What information do you think is most important here?
- Is any of this information new to you, or is it something you already knew?
- What would you do next?

*[Let them proceed.]*

---

### Recent care teams page

> **Pain point 2 (recent care teams):** Do the teams shown match who the Veteran would message about lab results?
> **Pain point 3 (provider confusion):** Can they identify the right team from what's shown?

- What do you see on this page?
- What do "recent care teams" mean to you?
- You said you wanted to ask about your lab results — is the team you'd message listed here?
- What teams would you expect to see here? Are these the teams you message most often?
- How many different care teams do you generally message?
- Are there care teams you frequently seek but cannot find when you see this page?

**Probe on "select a different care team" behavior:**

Is there a care team you might want to message that you *don't* see here? *(Have them name the care team.)* Great — what would you do if you want to message that care team but don't see them listed?

*[Allow them to click on the "a different care team" radio button and land on the "Select a care team" page.]*

---

### Select a care team page

> **Pain point 3 (provider confusion):** How do they find the right team in a potentially long, unfamiliar list?
> **Pain point 1 (flow complexity):** Does navigating to this second page feel like an extra burden?

- How do you find the care team you are looking for on this page?
  - probe: Do you search or scroll in the combo box?
  - probe: Do you rely on the care system to narrow your list of care teams?
  - probe: Does the length of the combo box list impact your ability to find the care team you're looking for?
- What helps you correctly identify the appropriate care team quickly and easily?
- Are there other categories of care teams besides recent care teams that you generally message? *(Probe: teams by specialty, by location, by how often you contact them?)*
- When you cannot find a care team in your dropdown, what actions do you take?

**Contact list awareness:**

What else do you notice on this page?

*[See if they notice the contact list link on their own. If they don't, point it out.]*

---

### Contact list page

> **Pain point 3 (provider confusion):** Do Veterans understand how the contact list works, and would they use it to manage which teams appear?

**Before they click on the contact list link:**
- How do you think the contact list works?

**After they click into it:**
- What do you think happens if you add or remove care teams from the contact list?
- Have you ever updated this? *(If yes)* What was that experience like? *(If no)* Is this something you'd use?

*[Have them navigate back to continue the message flow.]*

---

### Start message page (compose)

> **Pain point 1 (flow complexity):** Category selection adds another decision point before the Veteran can type their message.

Now you've reached the team you wanted. Walk me through what you would do on this page to write a message about your lab results. We're not actually going to send this message — this is just so I can see how this works for you.

*[Have the user pause at the category section:]*

- Thinking about your question about lab results, which category would you pick? How did you decide?
- What aspects of the categories do you find confusing?
- What choices do you default to when you are unsure what category to choose?
- Does choosing a category feel like a useful step, or does it feel like something extra between you and writing your message?

---

### Task 2 wrap-up

Thanks so much for walking me through that process.

- Thinking about everything you just did, from clicking "Start a new message" to getting here, how many steps did that feel like? Was that what you expected?
- If you could skip or combine any of those pages, which ones would you merge?
- Did the "recent care teams" shown match who you'd actually want to message about your lab results? 
- Overall, how easy or difficult was it to find the right care team to message?

We're now going to **delete this draft message**. Go ahead and discard it.

---

### Additional scenario (if time permits, ~3 minutes)

> **Moderator note:** Use this optional second scenario to test a different care team selection path. This is especially valuable if the Veteran selected from recent care teams in the first scenario and didn't need to use "select a different care team." Skip if running short on time.

**Scenario:** Now imagine you've been having some trouble sleeping and you want to reach out to your mental health provider or whoever on your care team handles that to ask about it. Can you show me how you would start that message?

*[Observe: Do they select from recent care teams, or do they need to search? Is the mental health team easy to find? Note any differences in behavior compared to the first scenario.]*

- How did finding this team compare to finding the one for your lab results?
- Was this team where you expected it to be?

*[Have them discard this draft as well.]*

# Section 3: Comparative Reflection (~5 minutes)

Now that you've seen your current experience on VA.gov and two different prototype versions, I'd like to get your overall thoughts.

### Flow complexity (Pain point 1)
1. **Which version felt the fastest** to get from "I want to send a message" to actually typing it? Why?
2. **Was there a version where the number of steps felt "just right"?** Which one, and what made it feel that way?

### Recent care teams (Pain point 2)
4. Across all the versions including your real account **how well did the system show you the teams you'd actually want to message?**
5. If you could design how your "go-to" care teams are surfaced, what would that look like? *(Probe: pinned favorites? Most recent? Most frequent? By specialty? Something else?)*
6. How important is it that the very first thing you see is the team you want vs. having to take one extra step to find them?

### Provider confusion (Pain point 3)
7. Across all the versions, **was there one where it was easiest to tell care teams apart and find the right one?** What made it easier?
8. Was the distinction between "care system" and "care team" clear in any version? What language or layout helped — or would help?
9. If you've ever messaged the wrong team by accident, what happened? What would have prevented that?

### Overall
10. If you could take the best parts from each version and combine them, **what would your ideal "start a new message" experience look like?**
