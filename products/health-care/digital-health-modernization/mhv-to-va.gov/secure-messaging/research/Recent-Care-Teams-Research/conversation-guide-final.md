## Intro – 5 minutes

Thanks for joining us today! My name is Espy and I'm a researcher with the VA. Today, I'd like to talk to you about how you send messages to your care team on VA.gov. Before we start, I have a few things that I want to go over with you:

- This session will take about 45 to 60 minutes. I’ll try to keep us on track and may occasionally move us to the next question or activity. If we finish early, I’ll give you that time back.  
- Your participation is completely voluntary. Whether you choose to participate or not will not affect your VA benefits in any way. If you'd like to stop at any point or skip a question, that is absolutely fine. Just let me know.  
- I’ll start by asking some questions about your experience and have you complete a few tasks around sending messages. For the first task, you’ll use your own My HealtheVet account on VA.gov. After that, we will switch over to a couple of prototypes. These are early designs we use to explore ideas and get feedback before making changes.  
- We’re interested in your honest feedback. There are no right or wrong answers. We’re not testing you, we’re testing the design. Please feel free to share anything that stands out to you, whether positive or negative.

## Task 2: Start a new message – 15 minutes

Now imagine you have a non-urgent question for your Patient Advocate and want to send them a message. Show me how you would go about doing that.

As you go, please think aloud and tell me what you are looking at, what you are thinking, and what you expect to happen.

*Observe entry point choice.*

---

### Interstitial page

*Observe silently first.*

*If they pause or spend time on this page:*
- What do you think this page is for?  
- What does “urgent” mean to you in this context?  

*If they move past this page quickly or skip it:*
- What do you remember about that page, if anything?  
- What did you think it was trying to tell you?  

---

### Care teams you recently sent messages to

*Observe silently first.*

- What are you noticing on this page?    
- Who do you think these options are?  
- Do you see who you were hoping to message here?  

*If not:*
- What would you do next?  

*Optional probe if needed:*
- How are you feeling about what to do from here?  

---

### Select care team page

*Observe search behavior first.*

- What are you noticing on this page?  
- What do you think you can do from here?  

- Go ahead and show me how you would try to find the person you’re looking for.  

*Observe: search vs scroll vs filters*

*If they pause or seem unsure:*
- What are you thinking right now?  

*If they do not find the expected care team:*
- What would you try next?  

*If links are not mentioned:*
- What else stands out to you on this page?  
- What do you think these links might help you do?  

---

### Start message page

*Observe silently first.*

- Walk me through how you would write your message.  

*Pause at category section:*
- How would you choose a category?  
- Is there anything in this section that feels unclear or confusing?  

*Have them discard draft.*

---

### Wrap-up

- What stood out to you most about that experience?  
- Was anything confusing or harder than you expected?  
- If you could change one thing, what would it be? Why?
