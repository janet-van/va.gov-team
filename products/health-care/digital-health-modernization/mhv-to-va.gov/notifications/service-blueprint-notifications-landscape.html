My HealtheVet Health Notifications: Landscape & Opportunities

What exists today across channels, where gaps exist, and where we can better serve Veterans on VA.gov

This artifact maps every existing health-related notification and in-app activity signal across the VA ecosystem — SMS, email, push, and in-app cards — for eight core health tools. Rows represent health tools; columns represent delivery channels, grouped as external outbound notifications (SMS/email/push) and on-screen in-app activity (VAHB mobile and VA.gov web). The rightmost column — In-App Activity (VA.gov / MHV Landing Page) — is highlighted as the primary opportunity space: it currently has the most gaps and is the focus of the MHV "recent activity" initiative.

Key Insights

💬

Secure Messages is the most complete

The only tool with coverage across nearly all channels — SMS, email, push, VAHB in-app, and web. Automatic and non-opt-outable across VistA and Oracle Health. The web surface exists but is incomplete (dot indicator only, no count).

💊

Medications has the biggest gap between what's possible and what's surfaced

VAHB mobile surfaces refill-ready prescriptions; web surfaces nothing. External notifications are fragmented — CBOC email is opt-in, VEText is facility-dependent. Some Veterans receive zero medication notifications of any kind.

🚫

Travel Pay, Medical Supplies, and Co-pays are notification dead zones

Minimal to zero coverage across all channels. Travel Pay has only a single VAHB activity card. Medical Supplies has nothing anywhere. Co-pays have a disabled email notification. All three are future opportunities requiring new work.

🌐

The web portal is the biggest single gap

The In-App (VA.gov) column has the most gaps of any channel. Only 2 of 8 tools surface any activity on web — both incomplete. This is the primary focus of the MHV recent activity initiative and represents the highest-leverage opportunity in the ecosystem.

Notification Platform Overview

VE
VEText

Facility-dependent SMS system built on CPRS/VistA data. Not available VA-wide — each facility opts in and budgets separately. Covers primarily VA appointments, community care scheduling, and prescription tracking. All Veterans are auto-opted in at the facility level; can opt out by replying STOP to received messages.

SMS only Facility-dependent VistA / CPRS data Auto-opted in Quiet hours 8am–8pm local time SMS STOP to opt out SMS START to re-enroll
VA
VA Notify

VA-wide notification platform supporting email, SMS, and push notifications (VAHB Mobile App). Template-driven passthrough — does not store Veteran data. Partners with VA Profile for preference management. SMS notifications can take up to 12 weeks to implement due to telecom compliance requirements.

Email + SMS + Push VA-wide Template-driven Requires API key + privacy review SMS: up to 12 weeks to implement VAHB push integration
☰
VA.gov Profile — Notification Settings

The authenticated settings page where Veterans manage notification preferences. Some tools are opt-in here (appointments reminders, medication shipment). Secure Messages is automatic and non-opt-outable — it does not appear in Profile settings at all. Note: opting out via SMS STOP and then re-checking a Profile box may not reactivate — Veterans may need to reply START to a prior message.

Preference management UI SM: automatic (not listed) Appointments: opt-in Meds shipment: opt-in SMS STOP may override Profile setting
