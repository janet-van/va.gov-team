Key Insights

💬 Secure Messages is the most complete

Secure Messages is the only health tool with coverage across all 6 channels — SMS, email, push, mobile in-app, and partial web. It's also the only tool with automatic, non-opt-outable notifications: users always know when they have a new message from a provider.

💊 Medications has the biggest gap between what's possible and what's surfaced

VAHB mobile already shows prescriptions ready to refill, but the web portal has zero medication activity. External notifications are fragmented across CBOC email and VEText SMS — meaning some Veterans get no medication notifications at all, depending on their facility.

🔇 Travel Pay, Medical Supplies, and Co-pays are notification dead zones

These tools have little to no notification coverage across any channel. Travel pay has an activity card on VAHB mobile but nothing else. Medical supplies and co-pays have nothing anywhere. These are future opportunities once the foundational recent activity system is in place on web.

🌐 The web portal is the biggest single gap

Column 6 (In-App Web) has the most gaps of any channel. Only 2 of 8 health tools surface any activity on VA.gov, and both are incomplete (dot-only indicator for messages; nearest-only for appointments, no count). This is the primary focus area: bringing the same visibility that VAHB mobile already provides into the MHV landing page.

Underlying Systems & Platforms

💬

VEText

Facility-dependent SMS system relying on CPRS data. Primarily VistA. Veterans are auto-opted-in; opt-out via SMS reply. Not all facilities have VEText enabled. Subject to legally mandated quiet hours (8 AM–8 PM facility local time). Powers most Appointments notifications — the richest single coverage area.

📧

VA Notify

VA-wide passthrough system supporting email, SMS, and push (VAHB mobile app). Template-driven — requires an API key + privacy review to create new notification types. Templates are not centrally managed: no single source of truth for what notifications exist or what they contain. Powers Secure Messages and medication shipping email notifications.

⚙️

VA.gov Profile — Notification Settings

Opt-in settings exist in VA Profile for select tools (e.g., medication shipping via email). Secure Messages is automatic and does not appear in Profile settings. VEText appointments are auto-enrolled with opt-out by SMS reply. This is where Veterans manage their notification preferences today — and the gap between what's there and what Veterans expect is itself a UX problem.
