# Cutover On-Call responsibilities for April Cutover

Note: This on call "process" is a one-off - we will have more streamlined on-call for the next migration.
Please bear with us for this first go.

## Key Points of contact (i.e. if your name is on [this canvas](https://dsva.slack.com/docs/T03FECE8V/F0AMTE398NM))

Key points of contact are responsible for fielding requests, intial triaging of reported issues, and knowing who to contact about what.

### Do:
* Add your phone number to the canvas, or provide it to a lead
* Check monitors before going to bed 4/10 and report out that things look good, or if there's a problem
* Check monitors after waking up 4/11 and report out that things look good, or if there's a problem
* Use succinct communication that conveys:
  * Description of the problem
  * Intent to address (or investigate)
  * Report back time
  * e.g. "One of our monitors is triggering - not an error yet, but I'm going to look into it. I'll update in 10 minutes.
* Overcommunicate

### Don't:
* Don't join the bridge call, unless something major is going on
* Don't work a problem in silence/silo - bring others in
