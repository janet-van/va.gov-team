# Medical Records Metrics & Feedback Study
**Analytics and Feedback Monitoring | February–April 2025**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains the research plan for an analytics and feedback monitoring study of Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov. Rather than moderated sessions, this study uses passive data collection — analytics dashboards and Veteran-submitted feedback — to understand how Veterans are using Medical Records, how long they spend in each domain, and what pain points they report.

**Status:** ✅ Study complete
