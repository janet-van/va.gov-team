# Medical Records Informational Interviews
**SME & Stakeholder Discovery Interviews | September–October 2022**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from informational interviews with VA subject matter experts — including MHV engineers, product owners, clinical staff, legal and privacy experts, and customer support staff. The research sought to understand the technical constraints, policy requirements, and known usability issues with MHV Medical Records ahead of the VA.gov redesign.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete
