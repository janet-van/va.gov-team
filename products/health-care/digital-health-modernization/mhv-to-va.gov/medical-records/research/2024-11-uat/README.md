# Medical Records User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
**User Acceptance Testing | November 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains the research plan template for User Acceptance Testing (UAT) of Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov. UAT is conducted to verify that the live product functions as expected for real Veterans before a broader release.

**Status:** ✅ Research plan complete
