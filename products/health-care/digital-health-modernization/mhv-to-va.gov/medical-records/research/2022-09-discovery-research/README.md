# Medical Records Technical Discovery Research
**Stakeholder & Technical Discovery Interviews | September 2022**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains the conversation guide for technical discovery interviews with VA staff about the Medical Records data architecture underpinning My HealtheVet (MHV). The research explored VistA data sources, Blue Button, VA Health Summary, the Cerner migration, and technical constraints affecting medical records functionality.
