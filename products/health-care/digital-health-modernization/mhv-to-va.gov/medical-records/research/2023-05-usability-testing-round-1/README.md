# Medical Records Usability Testing – Round 1
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing | May–June 2023**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from the first round of usability testing for Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov. The study tested a prototype with Veterans and Caregivers to assess whether they could locate their medical records across key domains (Labs & Test Results, Care Summaries & Notes, Allergies, Vaccines, Vitals, Health Conditions) and use the print and download functions.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete
