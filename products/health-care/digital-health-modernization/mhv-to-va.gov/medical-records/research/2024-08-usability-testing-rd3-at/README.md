# Medical Records Usability Testing – Round 3 (Including Assistive Technology)
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing & Interviews | August–September 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from the third round of usability testing for Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov. This round tested the live product (not a prototype) with both sighted Veterans and Veterans who use assistive technology. The study focused on Care Summaries & Notes, Lab and Test Results, and Health Conditions, and included generative questions about Veterans' medical records goals and terminology comprehension.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete

## How to Use This Research

**If you're designing:**
Start with [Research Findings](research-findings.md) to understand how Veterans used the live product to find Health Conditions, Care Summaries, and Lab Results — and where they struggled with medical terminology.

**If you're in product/leadership:**
Review the [Findings Readout (PDF)](findings-readout.pdf) for a high-level summary, then read [Research Findings](research-findings.md) for full detail and recommendations.

**If you're focusing on accessibility:**
Read [Conversation Guide – AT](conversation-guide-at.md) and [Research Findings](research-findings.md) together to understand how AT-dependent Veterans experienced the live product.

**If you're new to the project:**
Start with this README, then read [Research Findings](research-findings.md) to understand findings from testing the live Medical Records product with a diverse Veteran cohort.
