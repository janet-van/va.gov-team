# Medical Records Generative Research
**Semi-Structured Interviews with Card Sorting | October–November 2022**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from generative research conducted with Veterans and Caregivers. The study used semi-structured interviews and a moderated card sorting activity to understand how Veterans conceptualize medical records, what terminology they use, what actions they take with their records, and how their needs and goals influence their behavior on MHV. Findings informed the low-fidelity prototype for the Medical Records redesign on VA.gov.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete

## How to Use This Research

**If you're designing:**
Start with [Research Findings Readout](2022-11-medical-records-readout.md) to understand what terminology Veterans use, how they group medical records information, and what use cases drive their behavior. This directly informed the IA and labeling decisions in the redesign.

**If you're in product/leadership:**
Read the recommendations section of [Research Findings Readout](2022-11-medical-records-readout.md) for prioritized actions from this study.

**If you're new to the project:**
Start with this README, then read [Research Findings Readout](2022-11-medical-records-readout.md) to understand the foundational Veteran needs and mental models that shaped Medical Records on VA.gov.
