# Medical Records Usability Testing – Round 2 (Including Assistive Technology)
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing | March–April 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from the second round of usability testing for Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov. This round tested both sighted Veterans and Veterans who use assistive technology (AT), covering Care Summaries & Notes, Vaccines, Allergies, and Vitals. A focus was placed on identifying barriers for Veterans with cognitive disabilities and those using screen readers, screen magnification, or voice command.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete
