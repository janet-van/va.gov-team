# Medical Records Data Visualization Discovery
**Discovery Research | December 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains discovery research findings exploring how data visualization could improve the Medical Records experience on MHV on VA.gov. The research examines opportunities to present Veterans' health data in more meaningful, accessible visual formats.
