# Medical Records Recency Indicators – Notification Usability Study
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing with Concept Exploration | March–April 2026**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials for a usability study testing recency indicator designs for Medical Records on VA.gov. The study explores how Veterans define "new" or "recent" results after receiving notifications, and evaluates whether landing page indicator cards and list page badges help Veterans navigate to recently added records.

**Status:** 🔄 Study in progress
