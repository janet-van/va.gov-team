# Medical Records, Secure Messages & Medications – Filter Usability Study
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing | November 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from a usability study testing new filter functionality across three MHV on VA.gov products: Medical Records, Secure Messages, and Medications. The study assessed whether Veterans can use filters to find specific records, how they interpret filter options, and whether filters meet their needs across all three products.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete

## How to Use This Research

**If you're designing:**
Start with [Research Findings](research-findings.md) to understand how Veterans interpreted filter options in each product, where they struggled (especially Medications filters), and what use cases drove filter adoption.
