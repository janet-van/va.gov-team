# Medical Records Usability Testing – Round 4 (Assistive Technology)
**Moderated Remote Usability Testing | January 2025**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from the fourth round of usability testing for Medical Records on MHV on VA.gov, focused exclusively on Veterans who depend on assistive technology. This round tested newly released domains — Microbiology, Pathology, Radiology Results, Vitals, Allergies — as well as Medical Records Settings and the Download medical record feature, all within the live product environment.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete
