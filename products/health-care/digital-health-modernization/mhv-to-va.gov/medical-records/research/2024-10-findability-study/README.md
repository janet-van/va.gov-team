# Medical Records Findability Study
**Moderated Remote Contextual Inquiry | October 2024**

---

## What's In This Repository

This folder contains research materials and findings from a findability study comparing how Veterans locate health records in My HealtheVet Classic versus MyChart (non-VA). The study recruited Veterans who regularly use both portals and have a diabetes diagnosis, observing how they find specific records and what criteria they use to identify them.

**Status:** ✅ Research complete

## How to Use This Research

**If you're designing:**
Start with [Research Findings](research-findings.md) to understand what information Veterans use to identify records (especially date), and their need for sort, filter, and search functionality.

**If you're in product/leadership:**
Review the [Findings Readout (PDF)](Findability_Readout.pdf) for a high-level summary, then read [Research Findings](research-findings.md) for full detail and recommendations.

**If you're new to the project:**
Start with this README, then read [Research Findings](research-findings.md) to understand how Veterans navigate and search within their health records today.
