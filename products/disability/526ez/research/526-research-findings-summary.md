# 526EZ Research Findings Summary

This table summarizes all user research studies conducted for VA Form 21-526EZ (Application for Disability Compensation and Related Compensation Benefits). Each row captures the study topic, key findings, and links to the source documents in this repository.

> **Note:** Some older studies have findings stored in PDF format (Git LFS) and are not directly readable in this table. Where available, the most relevant markdown finding files are linked.

---

## Research Findings Table

| # | Study | Key Findings | Date & Source |
|---|-------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Early 526 Discovery** | Veterans prioritized accuracy over speed; free text fields caused anxiety about acceptance. Navigation was confusing (unclear what was clickable). Veterans wanted to add multiple conditions at once and expected to add evidence immediately after each condition. Lack of VA medical records was a "dead end" causing overwhelming confusion. | Sept 2017 — [synthesis-grids.md](archive/sep-2017/synthesis-grids.md) |
| 2 | **526 Full Flow Research — "Getting to the Form" & "Adding New Conditions"** | 4/5 participants found the form, but 1 was confused by terminology ("compensation" vs "disability"). Major confusion with body system selection for adding conditions: 4/5 had significant problems. PTSD was confused with physical conditions. 3/5 were confused about service-connected vs related conditions. | Mar 2018 — [themes.md](archive/mar-2018/themes.md) |
| 3 | **526 Usability Read-out — Pay Waiver & Disability Ratings** | Veterans preferred answering structured questions over typing descriptions. High stress around pay waiver decisions; recommendation: provide real dollar examples. Veterans confused about disability ratings (especially 100%); need guidance on whether filing for increase is productive. | Nov 2018 — [readout.md](archive/nov-2018/readout.md) |
| 4 | **Add New Condition Tree Testing** | 76–83% success rate across three unmoderated tasks with 21 participants. Some participants confused symptoms of one condition with another (e.g., tinnitus with PTSD). Some "wrong" paths made understandable sense, suggesting multiple valid routes to conditions. Overall groupings were clear but "something else" category needed improvement. | Feb 2019 — [read-out.md](archive/feb-2019/read-out.md) |
| 5 | **Automating Claim ID Type — AI API Testing** | AI API accuracy: 86% correct, 11% no match, 3% silently wrong. When matches made sense Veterans found it "cool," but 3% super-wrong errors (e.g., "loss of limb" → "hearing loss") severely eroded trust. **Recommendation:** Do NOT surface API matches to Veterans; use Veterans' own descriptions as labels. | Jul 2019 — [synthesis.md](archive/jul-2019/synthesis.md) |
| 6 | **PTSD-Focused Form 526 Research** | Study focused on PTSD claim filing experience in Form 526. Detailed findings available in PDF. | Mar 2021 — [Notes.md](archive/mar-2021/Notes.md) |
| 7 | **Usability Test of Form 526** | 8 remote moderated sessions testing overall UX, pain points, unclear content, and required information gaps. Detailed findings available in accompanying PDF. | Jul 2021 — [ResearchPlan.md](archive/July-2021/ResearchPlan.md) |
| 8 | **OCTO Benefits Portfolio Offsite Research** | Research to understand overall claimant experience with ~1,500 disability claims submitted daily. Goals: understand claims process conceptualization, identify friction points, build empathy, focus on speed + trust + automation impact. Hypothesis: significant friction areas exist that can improve Veteran trust and speed. | Jan 2023 — [2023_01_Benefits_Portfolio_Offsite.md](archive/2023_01_Benefits_Portfolio_Offsite.md) |
| 9 | **526 Medallia Survey Feedback Data White Paper** | 68 users received error messages requiring calls to Veterans Benefits Assistance, resulting in long hold times and frustration. Veterans struggle with updating auto-populated account information (address, service/discharge dates), causing errors. Users experienced "something went wrong" errors near ITF expiration. Users want to resume forms where they left off rather than at the beginning. Veterans may not understand that ancillary forms are part of the 526 digital form. | Jan–Mar 2023 — [Medallia-Survey-032023.md](2023-03-Medallia-Survey/Medallia-Survey-032023.md) |
| 10 | **526ez Landing Pages Redesign Research** | Research findings on redesigned landing pages. Detailed findings stored in PDF format (Git LFS). | May 2023 — [526 Landing Pages Redesign Research Findings.pdf](2023-05-526ezLandingPagesRedesign/526%20Landing%20Pages%20Redesign%20Research%20Findings.pdf) |
| 11 | **Form 526 Toxic Exposure Usability Research** | Study focused on toxic exposure (PACT Act) questions within Form 526. Research plan and conversation guide documented; detailed findings available in PDF format. | Jul 2023 — [research-plan.md](2023-07-Toxic-Exposure/research-plan.md) |
| 12 | **Conditions List Veteran Research** | Most participants found the type-ahead autosuggest component easy to use. Participants selected conditions from autosuggestions most of the time, but some had difficulty finding their conditions. Some didn't realize they could submit free text. Veterans know their conditions from experience but are uncertain about service connection. Technology isn't what holds Veterans back from digital filing — it's lack of trust and desire for VSO support. | Nov 2023 — [research-findings.md](CC-Team-Research/2023-10%20Conditions%20List/research-findings.md) |
| 13 | **Medallia Research Share Out** | Veterans face blockers requiring external support but find help difficult to get. Certain error messages lead Veterans to frustrating Contact Center encounters. Error messages block Veterans from filing. Veterans are misrouted to or stuck on the Reserves National Guard (RNG) page. Veterans reported evidence-related challenges throughout the application. | Oct 2023 — [2023-10 Research Findings.md](2023-10-Medallia-Research/2023-10%20Research%20Findings.md) |
| 14 | **526ez Shadowing Veteran Research** | Veterans' existing trust level in VA informs their reaction to uncertainty; those with lower trust abandon forms more easily. Service history section (especially Reserves/National Guard) is confusing for all who served in those branches. ITF errors lead to form exits and potential abandonment. Form navigation doesn't allow easy movement, making review difficult. Private medical record collection is burdensome. Lay statements add emotional and logistical complexity. Most participants didn't know to prepare documentation in advance. All questioned why bank account information was requested. Most expected confirmation email with claim copy post-submission. PTSD filing process is long and confusing. | Nov 2023 — [research-findings.md](2023-11-Shadowing-Research/research-findings.md) |
| 15 | **Intent to File (ITF) Research Summary** | Many Veterans are unable to start their 526 claim due to an ITF error with no clear resolution path. Veterans don't understand the various ways to express intent to file or how each method affects their benefits. ITF expiration deadlines are misunderstood — many believe they cannot file after one year when they can continue indefinitely. ITF information is inconsistent across VA.gov forms. | Feb 2024 — [ITF-Research-Summary.md](2024-02-ITF-Research/ITF-Research-Summary.md) |
| 16 | **526 Analytics — Calculating Drop-Off Rates** | A methodology for inferring drop-off rates was developed by identifying total pageviews per form page and subtracting subsequent pageviews. The Platform Analytics team created GA reports with previous page path data for pivot table analysis. Limitations: inflated pageview numbers due to inability to track unique users by IP address may skew drop-off rates lower. | Mar 2024 — [526_Analytics_Summary.md](2024-03-526-Analytics/526_Analytics_Summary.md) |
| 17 | **Improved Autosuggest Component Research** | Veterans significantly prefer selecting from the autosuggest list (perceiving them as pre-approved options) over free-text entry, which they worry could lead to claim denials. The autosuggest component substantially reduced time to add conditions. Veterans appreciated shorter examples lists; longer lists were too tedious. The save button's purpose was occasionally unclear — some thought it saved all conditions rather than just the current one. | May 2024 — [Report.md](CC-Team-Research/202403%20Improved%20Autosuggest/Report.md) |
| 18 | **Form 0781 Updates Concept Testing and User Research** | Veterans struggled to differentiate between evidence supporting mental health conditions versus general claim evidence, causing confusion about redundancy between 526 and 0781 evidence sections. Veterans expected all evidence to be provided sequentially rather than in separate sections. A hybrid approach is recommended: keep emotionally burdensome 0781 content together while consolidating evidence. ~10 Veterans appreciated mental health resources and 7 appreciated blue support alerts. | Aug 2024 — [Research Findings.md](2024-06%200781%20Research/Research%20Findings.md) |
| 19 | **526 Submission Status Research** | Veterans' mental models for claims processing don't align with the VA's electronic submission timeline. The submission received email clearly communicated final submission status; most didn't feel the need to check online status once receipt was confirmed. Veterans prefer digital resubmission of failed documents over mailing (safest/cheapest option). Missing touchpoints between submission and claim tracking availability cause worry. | Jul 2024 — [Research-findings.md](2024-07-Submission-Status/Research-findings.md) |
| 20 | **Conditions Page Assistive Technology Usability Testing** | Screen reader users faced substantial usability issues and cognitive load from reviewing long autosuggest lists, with critical focus trap and illogical focus order bugs on iOS. Screen magnifier users experienced minimal AT-specific challenges. Despite obstacles, AT participants demonstrated remarkable resilience and successfully completed most tasks. Recommendations: separate laterality options, fix iOS focus issues, add typo tolerance. | Aug 2024 — [researchreport.md](CC-Team-Research/2024-07%20Conditions%20Page%20Assistive%20Technology%20Testing/researchreport.md) |
| 21 | **Toxic Exposure Pre-Launch Review of Medallia Data** | Pre-launch task completion improved to 44.5% (from baseline 26%); intercept CSAT reached 41.9%. Key March 2023 findings still held true: certain error messages frustrate Veterans, BIRLS/EDIPI errors block submissions, evidence upload remains a persistent challenge. Veterans mentioned PACT Act 26 times in feedback, with 9 unable to complete their task. Existing navigation issues (Reserves National Guard page) could magnify cognitive burden with new Toxic Exposure questions. | Aug 2024 — [Toxic Exposure Pre-Launch Review of Medallia Data.md](2024-08-Toxic-Exposure/Toxic%20Exposure%20Pre-Launch%20Review%20of%20Medallia%20Data.md) |
| 22 | **Monitoring Toxic Exposure Release** | At 25% rollout, 48% of Veterans opted into the Toxic Exposure flow with minimal abandonment (1.3% average). Veterans call the Contact Center with high volume questions about the PACT Act (1,700+ entries). Overall 526 form abandonment (44.2%) remained lower than baseline (46.7%) and steady at ±2% throughout rollout. At 100% rollout, minimal direct Medallia feedback about toxic exposure questions, suggesting Veterans completed the flow without significant obstacles. | Aug–Nov 2024 — [TE-Monitoring.md](2024-08-Toxic-Exposure/TE-Monitoring.md) |
| 23 | **Condition Information Entry Assistive Technology Usability Testing** | The condition-by-condition approach significantly reduced cognitive load for AT users compared to the conditions-first approach. Separating laterality selection from condition name entry improved clarity. Focus management issues on iOS devices created significant barriers for screen reader users. Screen magnifier users found the experience comparable to non-AT users with minimal challenges. | Jan 2025 — [2024-11 Condition Info Entry Report.md](CC-Team-Research/2024-11%20Conditions%20Info%20Entry/2024-11%20Condition%20Info%20Entry%20Report.md) |
| 24 | **0781 Concept Testing Research** | Improvements to form flow address structural issues; Veterans found it clear and intuitive with better consolidated evidence sections. Mixed comprehension of supporting documents for mental health claims. Most destructive action modals were clear and appreciated. Opting out from Review & Submit was confusing — Veterans looked for "Delete" rather than "Edit." Veterans navigated multi-response add mode with ease but struggled with editing interactions. | Mar 2025 — [Research Findings.md](2025-02%200781%20Research/Research%20Findings.md) |
| 25 | **New or Increase at Condition Level Research** | Veterans found the new condition experience easy and intuitive; all completed core flows and described experience as fast. 3/7 had difficulty adding conditions secondary to previously rated conditions. Cause options improved but still caused difficulty. No one knows the dates their condition started or worsened; Veterans struggled with date entry requiring approximate dates. | Aug 2025 — [Research Findings - New or Increase at Condition level.md](CC-Team-Research/2025-06%20New%20or%20Increase%20at%20Condition%20Level/Research%20Findings%20-%20New%20or%20Increase%20at%20Condition%20level.md) |
| 26 | **Copy of Submission Research** | 5/8 participants intuitively interacted with the submitted data accordion to find submitted information. Almost all (7/8) found the most useful information is claim status in the green box. None (0/7) understood that confirmation page information disappears after leaving. All (8/8) successfully found specific submitted information within the accordion. All (7/7) saved submission copies via print function. Most (5/7) valued the saved copy as receipt and proof of submission. | Oct 2025 — [research-report.md](2025-09-Copy%20of%20Submission/research-report.md) |
| 27 | **0781 Evidence Upload Research** | Most participants struggled to understand differences between three upload sections (treatment records, mental health documents, other documents). 7/8 could navigate the document upload process successfully. 8/10 wanted more information up front about acceptable evidence types. Many misunderstood the treatment records choice page as being about uploading copies rather than giving VA access. 6/8 expected a summary page to confirm uploaded documents. | Oct 2025 — [0781 Evidence Upload Research Report.md](2025-10-0781-EvidenceUpload/0781%20Evidence%20Upload%20Research%20Report.md) |
| 28 | **686c Fully Digital Form Viewer MVP Usability Testing (Veterans)** | Only half of participants would print/download immediately post-submission, but all understood value for record-keeping. All agreed proposed designs achieve the goal of providing form copy; majority preferred VADS Minimal format for legibility. Veterans only want to see sections and questions they interacted with, not skipped optional sections. Nearly all understood "on this page" navigation but didn't find it necessary immediately post-submission. | Dec 2025 — [veteran-research-readout.md](2025-11-686c-Fully-Digital-Form-Viewer-MVP-Usability-Testing/veteran-research-readout.md) |
| 29 | **Supplemental Claims Design Directions — Usability** | Veterans strongly prefer viewing all conditions (rated and denied) on the "Add a condition" screen (Prototype Apple preferred 6-0 over Banana). Extremely difficult for Veterans to distinguish between Claim for Increase and Supplemental Claim; majority perceived both as "condition got worse." Most (6/10) expected evidence upload opportunity. "Add a condition" language caused confusion for Supplemental Claim/Claim for Increase scenarios. | Jan 2026 — [Research Findings - Usability.md](CC-Team-Research/2025-10%20526EZ%20Supplemental%20Claims%20Design%20Direction%20%26%20Mental%20Model%20Discovery/Research%20Findings%20-%20Usability.md) |
| 30 | **Supplemental Claims Design Directions — Mental Model Discovery** | Veterans approach benefits filing as a narrative case, not a procedural task; they want to tell their story and provide comprehensive evidence. Veterans must translate VA terminology to navigate the process; terms like "Supplemental Claim" create confusion. Veterans see VA as both adversary and partner; they distrust the system yet believe VA wants to help. Veterans naturally think in terms of medical conditions rather than claim types. | Feb 2026 — [Research Findings - Mental Model.md](CC-Team-Research/2025-10%20526EZ%20Supplemental%20Claims%20Design%20Direction%20%26%20Mental%20Model%20Discovery/Research%20Findings%20-%20Mental%20Model.md) |
| 31 | **526ez Shadowing Research** | Research plan and conversation guide prepared for upcoming shadowing study. Currently in planning phase. | Apr 2026 (Planned) — [research-plan.md](2026-04-526ez-shadowing/research-plan.md) |

## Key Themes Across All Studies

| Theme | Studies Where Found |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Trust & anxiety** — Veterans' trust in VA heavily influences form completion behavior | #1, #5, #12, #14, #29, #30 |
| **Terminology confusion** — VA terms don't match Veterans' mental models | #2, #4, #15, #29, #30 |
| **Error messages block filing** — System errors and unclear messages cause abandonment | #9, #13, #14, #15, #21 |
| **Evidence upload is burdensome** — Collecting and uploading evidence is a persistent pain point | #13, #14, #18, #27 |
| **Autosuggest for conditions works** — But needs refinement for accessibility and clarity | #12, #17, #20, #23 |
| **Confirmation & post-submission gaps** — Veterans want proof of submission and status updates | #14, #19, #26, #28 |
| **Accessibility (AT) barriers** — Screen reader users face significant challenges, especially on iOS | #20, #23 |
| **PTSD/mental health filing complexity** — The 0781 and mental health filing process is long and confusing | #6, #14, #18, #24, #27 |
| **Toxic Exposure (PACT Act) interest** — Strong Veteran demand and successful rollout with minimal abandonment | #11, #21, #22 |
| **Navigation & form structure** — Veterans want easier movement through the form and better expectation-setting | #1, #3, #14, #24 |
